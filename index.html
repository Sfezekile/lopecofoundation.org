BUILDING PEACE, ONE COMMUNITY AT A TIME.

The Lady of Peace Community Foundation (LOPECO) champions peacebuilding, honours everyday community heroes and empowers young people to lead change across Gauteng and beyond.

Support Our Work Our Initiatives

Our purpose

Dedicated to fostering meaningful connections between diverse communities, empowering people to lead local initiatives, and advocating for policies that promote safe communities. Peace is built by people doing the work, and we recognize those on the front lines, create platforms for youth voices, and push for peace-centred responses to the social and environmental challenges South Africans face every day.

Community Recognition

We honour community actors working to end gender-based violence and femicide (GBVF), so their work is seen, valued and supported.

Environmental Peacebuilding

We advocate for peace-centred climate action, including environmental education led by community peace clubs.

Youth Desk

A seat at the table for young people, building leadership, dialogue and peace-making skills for the next generation.

Our story

The Lady of Peace Community Foundation (LOPECO) promotes peace to end violence against women, children, and families - builds equal, resilient, and peaceful communities. Our vision is to create a connected, empowered and inclusive society where communities unite to build lasting peace, equality, and social justice.
